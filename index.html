Running a booth

Sample, then sell. Hand out a cookie or a pinch of granola, mention the one product that fits the season, and point to the bundles for gifts. Anything we don't have on the table is at 40centurygrain.com.
Split the jobs. One person samples and talks; the other runs the card reader and restocks. Swap every hour.
Local pickup. Customers can pick up scheduled orders at the Kitchen Incubator, 1741 Industrial Drive, Sterling. No retail sales there, pickups only.
After each event, jot down what sold out, what people asked for, and how many samples went out.
